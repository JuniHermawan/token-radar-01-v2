How the radar scores

implied value = TVL × 0.25 × growth × maturity × reach

Growth

The seven-day change, floored at −90%. Linear to +150%, then logarithmic: +150% and +4,448% are not scored the same — the tail adds 50 points per tenfold past the cap. Weighted 0.45.

Maturity

0.6 plus up to 0.4 over three years of running with no token. A protocol listed yesterday scores 0.6; one running three years scores 1.0. Unknown age counts as 365 days.

Reach

0.03 per extra chain beyond the first, up to five. A protocol on six chains scores 1.15; a single-chain one scores 1.00.

Driver

Each row's tag names the factor that carries it: Growth-led when the growth multiple is at least 1.25, Seasoned when maturity is at least 0.92, Multi-chain when reach is at least 1.09, otherwise Scale-led.

The same function scores every row — no protocol name is special-cased. The sandbox below recomputes every row live; the submitted board uses the radar-3 constants above.

How this radar is built

Every row is one protocol from the DeFiLlama protocol index as of 2026-11-14, with at least $5M of TVL. A row is ranked when its record carries no token (no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol) and it is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades, nor a versioned pool. The watchlist is the floor, not the board: the board ranks the whole rankable universe. Live-token protocols are set aside and shown with their market cap, never mixed into the ranked board.

Each farm plan is eight steps, derived from the protocol's own record: the primary and second actions from its category, the gas from its first chain, the capital from its TVL tier ($250 at $1B, $150 at $100M, $75 at $10M, $50 below), the hold from its age (60 days below 180, 45 below 365, 30 older), and a risk note specific to its category. Nothing here is a promise of an airdrop — a protocol with no token may never launch one.
